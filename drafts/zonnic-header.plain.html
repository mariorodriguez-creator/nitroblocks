Zonnic chrome (header + footer)

Local draft for the full Zonnic shell: logo, healthcare link, main navigation, mobile menu with overlay, and the Zonnic footer (columns, accordion on narrow viewports). Scoped for migration POC (no QuitZone promo, locale, or account in this draft).

Preview with aem up --html-folder drafts at http://localhost:3000/drafts/zonnic-header. scripts.js injects header-variant, footer-variant (zonnic), and footer → /footer when those metas are absent. Zonnic footer data is plain sections in footer.plain.html at the site root (same EDS pattern as nav.plain.html for the header); the footer block parses it and builds BAT markup.

Header baselines: site-migration/www.zonnic.ca/visual-baselines/product-header/ (see scripts/zonnic-capture-header-poc.mjs). Logos and health-warning art live under blocks/header/zonnic-assets/; the footer white logo is under blocks/footer/zonnic-assets/.
