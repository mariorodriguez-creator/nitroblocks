FAQ

IS ZONNIC A NATURAL HEALTH PRODUCT (NHP)?

Regulatory classification varies by market. Refer to the product label and Health Canada registration details for the most accurate statement for Canada.

CAN I USE ZONNIC IF I DO NOT SMOKE?

ZONNIC is intended for adult nicotine users. If you do not currently use nicotine, you should not start.

HOW LONG SHOULD I KEEP A POUCH IN PLACE?

Most adults use a pouch for up to 30 minutes. Follow the directions on your package and talk to a clinician if you are unsure.
