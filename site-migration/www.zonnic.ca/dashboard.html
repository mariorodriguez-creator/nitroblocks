Structural Decisions
Must be resolved before development starts
1. Age Gate: Auto-block vs Authored Block
The age gate is identical on every page and is a regulatory requirement. Should it be injected by scripts.js (auto-block) or authored in each page?
↪ Recommended: Auto-block in scripts.js
2. E-Commerce: External Redirect vs Embedded Cart
Products are currently sold through Felix / LiveWell pharmacy partners. Should EDS replicate the redirect model or embed a cart widget?
↪ Recommended: External redirect for MVP (reduces complexity significantly)
3. Newsletter: Fragment vs Auto-block vs Footer
Newsletter signup appears on every page above the footer. Should it be a reusable fragment, an auto-block, or part of the footer?
↪ Recommended: Fragment (author-controlled, DRY)
4. Registration Form: Native vs External Embed
Complex form with address autocomplete, password management, and account creation. Rebuild natively or embed from identity provider?
↪ Recommended: External embed (Salesforce/IdP)
5. Bilingual Content: Separate Trees vs Language Toggle
Site serves EN and FR at /ca/en/ and /ca/fr/ respectively. Use separate content trees or single tree with variants?
↪ Recommended: Separate content trees (matches source URL structure)
