# Pattern Inventory

**Source site:** https://www.zonnic.ca
**Templates analyzed:** 15
**Total content sequences found:** 52
**Unique patterns after deduplication:** 18

---

## Pattern 1: Age Gate Modal

**Description:** Full-screen overlay that blocks page access until the user confirms they are 18+ and selects a Canadian province. Includes logo, language toggle, province dropdown, confirm/deny buttons, and error state.
**Frequency:** 15 templates (all pages)
**Locations:** All pages — renders before any content is visible
**Variations:** None — identical across all pages

---

## Pattern 2: Health Warning Banner

**Description:** Full-width regulatory image banner showing a health warning about Nicotine Replacement Therapy. Responsive images for desktop and mobile breakpoints.
**Frequency:** 15 templates (all pages)
**Locations:** All pages — immediately below age gate, above header
**Variations:** None — identical across all pages

---

## Pattern 3: Site Header with Navigation

**Description:** Sticky header with logo (left), hamburger menu (mobile), primary navigation (7 items with icons), language/region selector modal, account login dropdown with slide-in form, healthcare practitioners link, and mini cart slide-in overlay.
**Frequency:** 15 templates (all pages)
**Locations:** All pages
**Variations:** Minor — some pages show login state vs logged-out state

---

## Pattern 4: Site Footer

**Description:** Multi-column footer with social media follow prompt, about text, quick links (2 columns), legal links, support contact info (phone + ticket link), business hours, social media icons, and Zonnic logo.
**Frequency:** 15 templates (all pages)
**Locations:** All pages
**Variations:** Homepage includes additional "Shop online via Felix" CTA

---

## Pattern 5: Newsletter Signup Bar

**Description:** Centred heading ("Sign up for our newsletter"), subheading, single email input field with submit button. Full-width section with background color.
**Frequency:** 15 templates (all pages)
**Locations:** All pages — appears above footer
**Variations:** None

---

## Pattern 6: Full-Width Hero Banner

**Description:** Full-width background image with overlaid heading text. Responsive images for desktop/mobile. Sometimes includes subtitle paragraph and CTA button(s).
**Frequency:** 12 templates
**Locations:** Homepage (variant), Blog Listing, Product Listing, FAQ, Healthcare Landing, Healthcare Article, Contact Us, What Is Zonnic, Why Zonnic, Real People Landing, Testimonial Detail, Store Locator
**Variations:** Some heroes have CTA buttons and body text; some are image-only with heading. Homepage uses a product carousel instead of static hero.

---

## Pattern 7: Product Carousel

**Description:** Rotating carousel of product "masthead" cards with large product can image, tagline heading, and "Learn more" CTA. Navigation arrows and dot indicators. Uses Slick slider.
**Frequency:** 3 templates
**Locations:** Homepage §1, Product Detail §2, Store Locator §2
**Variations:** Homepage shows 3 products; Product Detail shows "other flavours"; Store Locator shows online pharmacy partners instead of products

---

## Pattern 8: Article/Blog Card Grid

**Description:** Grid of content cards, each with thumbnail image, title heading, short excerpt paragraph, and "Read More" CTA link.
**Frequency:** 5 templates
**Locations:** Homepage §5 (3 cards), Blog Listing §2 (28 cards), Blog Article §4 ("More From Us", 2 cards), Healthcare Landing §2 (8 cards), Healthcare Article §3 (2 cards)
**Variations:** Card count varies (2-28). Some grids have a section heading and "View All" CTA link. Layout is 3-column desktop / stacked mobile.

---

## Pattern 9: FAQ Accordion

**Description:** Expandable question/answer pairs under an "FAQ" heading. Each question is clickable to reveal the answer. Usually 3 questions shown with "View All" CTA to full FAQ page.
**Frequency:** 6 templates
**Locations:** Blog Article §3, Product Detail §7, What Is Zonnic §8, Why Zonnic §7, Testimonials Landing §6, Testimonial Detail §6
**Variations:** Questions change per context but structure is identical. Always 3 questions.

---

## Pattern 10: Video Embed

**Description:** Embedded video player showing testimonial stories or product instruction videos. Inline within page content.
**Frequency:** 5 templates
**Locations:** Homepage §6, Product Detail §6, Testimonials Landing §3 (4 videos), Testimonial Detail §3 (2 videos), What Is Zonnic §5
**Variations:** Count varies from 1-4 per page. Some are instructional, some are testimonial stories.

---

## Pattern 11: Testimonial Quote Carousel

**Description:** Carousel of testimonial cards, each featuring a user's photo, name, and quote. Navigation dots or arrows. Links to individual testimonial detail pages.
**Frequency:** 3 templates
**Locations:** Why Zonnic §4, Testimonials Landing §4, Testimonial Detail §5 ("More Stories")
**Variations:** Some show full quotes, some show preview cards. Number of items varies.

---

## Pattern 12: Icon + Text Benefit Grid

**Description:** Grid of cards (typically 6), each with an icon/image, short heading, and brief description. Communicates product features or category navigation.
**Frequency:** 3 templates
**Locations:** What Is Zonnic §6 (product benefits), FAQ §2 (category navigation), Contact Us §2 (contact methods)
**Variations:** FAQ uses it for navigation (with "View All" CTAs); What Is Zonnic uses it for feature highlights; Contact Us uses it for contact methods with action CTAs.

---

**Frequency:** 1 template
**Locations:** Product Listing §2
**Variations:** Single cans and bundle options

## Pattern 14: Product Detail Hero

## Pattern 15: Interactive Store Locator Map

**Locations:** Store Locator §1
**Variations:** None

## Pattern 16: Lifestyle/Motivational Blurb Cards

**Description:** Grid or carousel of image + text "blurb" cards with lifestyle photography and short motivational text. Used to communicate brand values and quit-smoking motivation.
**Frequency:** 3 templates
**Locations:** Homepage §3 (3 tall info cards), Why Zonnic §5 (6 "break the pattern" cards), Testimonial Detail §2 (3 benefit cards)
**Variations:** Card count varies (3-6). Some are carousels on mobile, static grid on desktop.

## Pattern 17: Social Media / Instagram Section

**Description:** Section featuring Instagram photos of users with products, "Let's get social" heading, and social follow icons/links.
**Frequency:** 1 template
**Locations:** Homepage §4
**Variations:** None

## Pattern 18: Multi-Step Registration Form

**Description:** Large multi-section form with: account details (name, email, DOB), billing address (with autocomplete), sign-in credentials (password), marketing consent, and smoker status dropdown.
**Frequency:** 2 templates
**Locations:** Sign Up §1 (full page), All pages (as slide-in modal from header)
**Variations:** Full-page version vs modal/slide-in version
