What are the benefits of using ZONNIC to help you quit smoking?

ZONNIC is a Nicotine Replacement Therapy. As such, it delivers nicotine to your body, which increases the chances of quitting smoking successfully, whether that be gradually or right away. ZONNIC is a nicotine replacement therapy that can be used anytime, anywhere to temporarily control nicotine cravings fast. ZONNIC pouches are discreet and won’t be noticed by those around you when tucked under your upper lip. Our pouches contain no tobacco and don’t produce tar or carbon monoxide, and therefore don’t come with the same risks as cigarette smoke. Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/smoking-tobacco/quit-smoking/how.html

What can ZONNIC be used for?

ZONNIC can be used by those looking to kick their smoking habit, whether that be all at once or gradually. Although smoking cessation is preferrable, ZONNIC can also be used to temporarily avoid smoking – for example, around others, in smoke-free areas, or in airplanes.

Why should I use Nicotine Replacement Therapy (NRT)?

Nicotine Replacement Therapy (NRT) is a proven method that can help the odds of quitting success. It can also help ease physical withdrawal symptoms when you start to cut back, so you can focus on you without having to worry about cravings.
